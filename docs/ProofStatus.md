independent matrix batches and view-specific errors. The adaptive affine-projection development is

The block-binary development checks the finite computational reduction without adopting the
lattice estimator's heuristic attack costs as a theorem. Its strongest theorem uses one signed
matrix distinguisher and one randomized row reduction, so it does not separately absolute-value the
two masking sides or the individual row gaps. It also exposes the unsplit distance
`jointStatisticalGap`, which may be smaller than the sum of noise absorption and extraction.

The strongest split analytic route no longer assumes any bound on `noiseAbsorptionGap`. It proves
that gap is at most `nonlinearNoiseAbsorptionCost`, the exact finite expectation of a multiplicative
product bound over the actual key and narrow-error matrix. Each per-sample TV is evaluated after the
selected narrow errors have been summed, so additive cancellation is retained. The product remains
on the probability scale, and the formal dominance theorem
`nonlinearNoiseAbsorptionCost_le_min_expected_blocks` proves it is never worse than
`min(1, m * kℓ / (ℓ+1) * δ_scalar)`. The older shift-moment result is only an optional coarser
corollary.

`nonlinearNoiseAbsorptionCost` is an upper bound, not an asserted equality with
`noiseAbsorptionGap`: conditioning on the shared context, deterministic postprocessing, and the
universal independent-product overlap inequality can each be strict for a particular distribution.
The exact quantities remain `noiseAbsorptionGap` and, most sharply for the combined statistical
hop, `jointStatisticalGap`. Likewise, the smaller PMF `convolutionDistance` is applicable when the
summed shift is hidden; it is not substituted into the transcript reduction, where the public
challenge can carry information about the narrow-error matrix.

an exact ideal *discrete* specialization suitable for a `ZMod q` model: sample the centered integer
Gaussian with standard deviation `αq`, then reduce modulo `q`. This matches the direct standard-
deviation convention of the TFHE parameter entries in `lattice-estimator`; heuristic attack costs
from the estimator are not treated as proof assumptions. Because ideal discrete Gaussians have
infinite support while `ProbComp` computations have finite support, the formalization does not make

The shared-IKSK comparison is stronger than merely reusing the shared-randomness LWE assumption.

entries for `unusedPrefix` leaves exactly the optimized IKSK for the nested key

coordinate of `retainedKey`; its message encoder is typed to receive only the independently
sampled suffix. The scalar error and unused-prefix samplers are required to be total only where
their discarded draws must be marginalized in `ProbComp`'s subprobability semantics. This is an

full-IKSK adversary class contains the suffix-projection reduction. BRK ciphertexts are not in
these projection games.

`TFHE.BootstrappingSecurity` additionally proves that native `Z + μH` TGSW sampling is exactly a
fresh direct module-LWE batch carrying the gadget-phase messages. The mask-coordinate rows carry
the bilinear cross-key values `-(s_j * (μ * g_l))`; the last block carries the affine values
`μ * g_l`. This equality is lifted through the real KSK and arbitrary downstream continuation,
with no statistical loss. The remaining premise is therefore the explicit
`DirectBilinearHardAgainst` KDM assumption, not an unspecified structured-ciphertext gap.
`oneTimeHardAgainst_of_directBilinear_and_jointLwe` composes it with the checked joint-LWE bound.
`TFHE.MultiQuerySecurity` additionally carries the same argument through a fixed vector of
`queryCount` challenges chosen after the cloud key is visible. Its second LWE block has exactly
that many rows, the cloud-key replacement occurs once for the entire vector, and equal scalar
noises give conventional batch LWE on `keySwitchSamples + queryCount` rows.
`TFHE.AdaptiveEncryptionSecurity` supplies the sequential strengthening: the adversary is a
query-bounded oracle computation and may choose later message pairs from earlier ciphertexts.
The checked source reduction uses one eager LWE row per charged query, and its uniform branch is
proved bit-independent for the complete adaptive computation.
`TFHE.CutCycleSecurity` further proves that after the opposite KSK messages have been zeroed, BRK
replacement reduces to two parallel binary-secret module-LWE advantages by a shared uniform
transcript translation. Thus both post-cut directions are standard LWE/module-LWE obligations;
the direct-bilinear premise is localized to the first replacement while the two-way cycle remains
intact. The abstract and concrete alternative-order theorems additionally show that selecting the
KSK as the first replacement yields an equivalent intact-cycle premise up to those same post-cut
terms, and provide a complete one-time bound in that ordering.
Ordinary LWE/RLWE and the direct-affine theorem do not by themselves establish this bilinear KDM
assumption. The exact scope and next steps are documented in `docs/TFHECircular.md`.

### Exact selected-diagonal rank-failure route

The selected self-correlated diagonal now also has an exact alternative to its finite-fiber
bound. Once the uniform difference ciphertext is fixed, bijectivity of its induced row operator
lifts to bijectivity on the complete public challenge matrix, so the uniform mask law is
transported exactly. Averaging zero cost on good differences and the universal unit bound on bad
differences proves that the whole mask hop is at most the probability of a non-bijective row
operator under the actual difference distribution. Combining this with the existing mixed
transformed-error distance gives the rank-sharp diagonal theorem and a whole-key certificate
constructor. This is valid over the production power-of-two coefficient ring and uses neither a
finite-field assumption nor a linear digitizer. The induced operator now has a canonical square
matrix whose entries are proved to be the identity plus the candidate-signed gadget digits.
Bijectivity is proved equivalent—not merely related—to its determinant being a unit, so the exact
bad-rank probability is exactly a determinant non-unit probability. A bounded whole-key
constructor accepts any candidate-wise estimate of this algebraic event. The exact-capacity
even-base case is now resolved negatively: a checked reduction modulo two and evaluation at
`X = 1` maps the row matrix to an exactly uniform square binary matrix. Its rank failure is at
least `1/2`, which forces the original determinant non-unit probability and the rank-sharp
diagonal loss to be at least `1/2` for either candidate. This rules out the determinant route for
the native power-of-two family. The direct side-information collision hybrid is now connected to
the whole-key certificate in exact side-wise and normalized Pearson forms. The Pearson term is now
also bounded by one source-error-independent total rectangular-pair excess, divided by the exact
difference/challenge normalization. The free native-image lift now bounds that total rank by rank
and connects the resulting normalized expression to the whole-key certificate. The equal-
difference slice is now proved to force this global relaxation above the original square
rank-failure probability, hence above `1/2` at exact capacity with even base. The global
pair-collision convenience premise is therefore formally non-negligible for the canonical family.
This does not lower-bound the exact retained-fiber loss. The equal-difference slice now has an
exact denominator-preserving row-normalized moment identity and a canonical `2^-N` upper bound
obtained from the centered support of every actually reachable reconstructed row target. The
distinct retained-fiber moment is now an exact centered row-Fourier product. Its unavoidable
source-error Fourier mode is split off with exact transformed-error phase; exploiting that phase
and bounding the remaining minor-weighted coefficients remain open, while the direct native
CircLWE/KDM route remains a sound conditional security endpoint.
