# Requested papers

The following papers would be useful for checking whether known random-subset-sum algorithms can
instantiate the efficient anchored binary Ring-ISIS compiler. I could locate bibliographic pages
but not an openly accessible copy of the article itself.

- Russell Impagliazzo and Moni Naor, *Efficient Cryptographic Schemes Provably as Secure as
  Subset Sum*, Journal of Cryptology 9(4), 1996, pp. 199–216,
  DOI 10.1007/BF00189260.
    - ../refs/BF00189260.pdf
- Chris Monico, *Cryptanalysis of a Hash Function, and the Modular Subset Sum Problem*, Groups,
  Complexity, Cryptology 11(1), 2019, pp. 17–23, DOI 10.1515/gcc-2019-2001.
